## Resumen

Fuente tipificada como [psychom, on_topic]. Síntesis del abstract/contenido disponible: **OBJECTIVE:** A new condition, 'child affected by parental relationship distress' (CAPRD), was introduced in the DSM-5. A relational problem, CAPRD is defined in the chapter of the DSM-5 under 'Other Conditions That May Be a Focus of Clinical Attention.' The purpose of this article is to explain the usefulness of this new terminology. **METHOD:** A brief review of the literature establishing that children are affected by parental relationship distress is presented.
